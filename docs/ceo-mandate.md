# Promoted: the owner and the agentic CEO

The owner sets direction and resources. Promoted manages day-to-day engineering within that mandate and reports what changed, why, what it cost, and what needs an owner decision. The CEO framing is a product interface, not unrestricted authority for a model.

## Owner briefing

The primary view should answer: what matters next; what shipped; what remains blocked; what customers are asking for; how resources were spent; which decisions need the owner. Engineering attempts and gate logs remain inspectable beneath this report.

Keep separate records for feedback, backlog proposals, approved work, engineering attempts, verified releases, expenditure and decisions. A task completed by a model is not a released feature. A reported customer problem is not automatically a defect. A model's claim of success is not gate evidence.

## Mandate and escalation

The future mandate specifies permitted projects/paths, routine-work categories, protected policies, deadlines, per-task and period budgets, provider limits and release destinations. Routine bounded repairs can proceed without human interruption. Unknown authority or insufficient budget holds the task; it never silently expands permissions.

Escalate investment beyond the approved threshold, substantial new features, technology/framework changes, product pivots, protected gate adoption and release-destination changes. Decisions present the proposed outcome, evidence, alternatives, estimated cost range, scope, rollback plan and exact proposal revision. Owner approval must bind that revision, expire appropriately and reserve the authorized amount atomically. Stale approval cannot authorize a different proposal.

The owner's example of EUR 100 for a feature is illustrative, not an activated spend limit or authorization. No numeric mandate is configured yet. Human approval permits an action within policy; it cannot turn a failed correctness gate into a pass.

## Engineering P&L

Show approved budget, reserved commitments and observed expenditure separately. Attribute usage to provider, task and attempt, retain reported/estimated/unknown reliability and timestamps, and reconcile cumulative provider reports without double counting. Missing token counts, prices or currency rates remain unknown. Do not convert ACUs into token counts or money without verified rates.

Costs can later include model usage, execution infrastructure and other explicitly integrated services. Revenue or business benefit requires an attributed external measurement; do not invent ROI, monetary savings or a profit figure from shipped task counts. Until revenue is connected, label the view engineering spend, not a complete business P&L.

## Implementation boundary

The initial owner report provides real incident-derived backlog counts for the visible snapshot, known implementation status, integration readiness and gate inventory. Feedback ingestion, budget ledger, autonomous prioritization, policy decisions and financial reconciliation are planned. Their UI states explicitly report not connected/not configured; they do not show fabricated zero spend or fictional customer feedback.

Next delivery packages: feedback inbox and deduplication; prioritized proposals with evidence; versioned mandate and budget reservations; decision queue with optimistic revision checks; provider-backed task execution; verified releases; reconciled periodic owner reports. These extend the control plane without changing the frozen v1 gate-evidence contract.
