# Office interaction review

The scene must be judged in motion and at every inspection distance. A generated image or a successfully loaded GLB is not evidence of a finished interactive component.

## Problems corrected

- Duplicate lounge instance crowded the engineering area: one lounge instance remains.
- Generated characters were translated while an idle clip played. Walk cycles now follow distance travelled, with acceleration, turning, relaxed arms and pauses. Routes remain ambient presentation, not evidence of agents working.
- The static vault could not open. Its front has been articulated, the noisy shell boundary repaired with a bevelled housing, and the live finance surface placed inside. Closing inspection closes the same door.
- The generated conveyor hid the moving belt. A registered belt surface now moves during active verification; an active scanner accompanies it. Failed checks stop movement. Gate signs have physical housings and retain their own evidence inspection targets.
- All inspections flattened the camera. Station-specific angles preserve depth; finance frames the whole vault and QA frames the full line. The tour adds gentle changes of angle as well as pan and zoom.
- The ticker expanded into a television. It remains a horizontal ribbon, with short recorded metrics and a subtle LED grid, including during inspection.
- Oversized engineering furniture and the muted room palette weakened the composition. The desk scale, cobalt wall, contrasting brand band, rug and lighting have been revised.

## Verification

Browser checks cover opening and closing the generated safe, conveyor movement during the labelled demo, stopping at failure, all six inspection views, and reduced-motion behavior. Screenshots are local review artifacts, not published product evidence. Model and operator tests remain separate from these visual checks.

## Remaining visual limitations

Generated meshes still contain reconstruction noise, especially upholstery and fine hardware. Character gait is procedural, not a motion-captured walk with foot IK. These assets remain visual-review previews. Final acceptance requires watching a full circulation loop and inspecting the actual scene, not just its room concept or asset thumbnail.

Do not convert an absent cost, unreported usage, idle machine or failed gate into fictional success for visual effect. The labelled demo exists to show active motion without modifying controller records.
